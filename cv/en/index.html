2026

Evaluating Qwen3-ASR for Korean Speech Recognition

  • Compared 0.6B and 1.7B models on 10 Korean evaluation sets, analyzing character error rate (CER) across inference settings.
  • Compared language, maximum generation length, temperature, and repetition penalty to derive reproducible default inference settings.
2026Present

DCASE 2026 Task 6 · Audio Moment Retrieval

  • Reproduced the official baseline and full evaluation pipeline for locating audio segments matching natural-language queries.
  • Experimenting with MS-CLAP audio-language representations and DETR-based temporal localization models.
20252026

Academic-Regulation QA Data and Error-Correction Training

  • Designed and reviewed 9,958 synthetic examples with PASS, FAIL, and OOS labels; trained Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B using 4-bit QLoRA.
  • Measured a FAIL correction rate of 0.9897 with ground-truth review signals provided; analyzed the error-review bottleneck in end-to-end inference.
